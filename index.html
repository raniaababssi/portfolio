Rania Ababssi is an architecture student interested in the relationship between architecture, culture, technology, and communities.

Through academic and cultural projects, she explores how architectural design can respond to people, their environment, and the identity of a place.

Her experience also extends into academic research, including adaptive reuse, sustainable materials, building performance, fire safety, and evacuation simulation.
